Status

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

The trusted rental watch and platform. You put in a postal code or area and it shows what’s around — beginning as a rental watch on the HuntSteals pattern: area/postal code plus criteria (basement, legal suite, price line, pet policy), with new matching listings surfaced as a checkable page. The trust layer is the product: rentals are a scam-dense market, so every listing is labelled with its source, verification status, and duplicate or suspected-fake flags — the Dawn truth-label discipline pointed at rent.

END GOAL

To be the platform people trust for renting: authenticated and original. Built by reusing Dawn assets that already exist — the watch pattern (HuntSteals), the labels (DawnCanon), the gates (DawnGateWatch), the languages (DawnI18n). Founding market fact (Livingston, Calgary, 2026-11-21): legal basement suites cluster at $950–$1,350/month.

ROADMAP

  • Watch first — dogfood #1 is the founder’s own move: his personal Livingston watch goes live first, on his Jan-2027 move timeline.
  • Sources: RentFaster, Zumper, Kijiji, Rentals.ca, REW.
  • The trust layer: verified / original / authenticated labelling on every listing.
  • Platform grilling follows — fee model, verification depth, which market first. The founding fee speculation (1% light-touch; 3–10% only where Dawn does real work) is unvalidated and must be earned by work actually done.

Roadmap drawn from the founding brief’s formalisation and next step (2026-11-21; grilling not yet done, as recorded).
